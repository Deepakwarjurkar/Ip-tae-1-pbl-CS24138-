How DCP works

The mathematical pipeline behind the dehazing process.

01

Dark Channel

Identify pixels with very low intensity in at least one color channel.

02

Atmospheric Light

Estimate the global atmospheric light responsible for the haze.

03

Transmission

04

Scene Recovery

Recover the underlying haze-free scene using the atmospheric scattering model.

ATMOSPHERIC SCATTERING MODEL
I(x) = J(x)t(x) + A(1 − t(x))

The model describes the formation of a hazy image from scene radiance, transmission and atmospheric light.
